1 Unpack & Launch SIGHTLINE™ Standard & PRO
⏱️ ~1 Minute
🪟 On Windows 10 / 11 (Uses Built-In Edge WebView2)

1. Locate your downloaded SIGHTLINE_PRO_Windows.zip (or SIGHTLINE_Standard_Windows.zip).

2. Important: Right-click the .zip file and click Extract All... into a normal folder (e.g. C:\SIGHTLINE). Never double-click Sightline.exe from inside an unextracted zip preview, as it needs its _internal folder beside it!

3. Open the extracted folder and double-click Sightline.exe. (If Windows SmartScreen shows a first-run publisher prompt, click More info → Run anyway).

🍎 On Apple macOS (Uses Built-In Apple WKWebView)

1. Double-click SIGHTLINE_PRO_Mac.dmg (or SIGHTLINE_Standard_Mac.dmg).

2. Drag SIGHTLINE PRO.app into your Applications folder (you can also copy the included Projects demo folder to your Documents).

3. First Launch Only: Open Applications, Right-Click (Control + Click) on SIGHTLINE PRO.app, and click Open. After the first launch, it opens normally with a double-click.

5 Start Your Own Novel, Screenplay, or Game World
⏱️ ~2 Minutes
Path A: Guided Blank Starter Standard & PRO

1. Open the Left Console (▶) → 📂 Project Switcher.

2. Type a new folder path (e.g. Projects/My_Novel) and click Load / Scaffold Project (0 LLM). SIGHTLINE™ creates a self-guiding 2-character starter scene (PROTAGONIST & RIVAL).

3. Click Cast (2) ▾ in the Top Bar → click ✎ Voice to rename your characters and set their speech cadence & Hard Never-Rules, or click + Spawn Character.

4. Click ✎ Edit on any card in the Right Inspector to customize starting locations, items, and secrets in 0.0s, and click ▤ on the Left Rail to add World Bible lore.

Path B: 1-Click Synopsis Auto-Ingest PRO ONLY

Already have a story outline, character bible, or rough Chapter 1 written?

1. Inside 📂 Project, Demo, Ingest & Backup, click ⚡ Auto-Ingest Synopsis / Chapter (1 GM).

2. Paste your raw synopsis or opening chapter and click ⚡ Generate Turn 0, World Bible & Voice Anchors.

3. In one single AI call, SIGHTLINE™ PRO automatically builds your opening cast, locations, inventories, secrets, blind spots, World_Bible.md, and Voice_Anchors.md!

📌 Using SIGHTLINE™ Standard? Use Path A to scaffold and edit your cast at 0 LLM cost. In SIGHTLINE™ PRO, you also unlock Path B (1-Click Auto-Ingest), 🗑️ 2-Mode Character Deletion (Forward Exit vs. Full Project Purge), 📦 1-Click Project .zip Export, and 🕰️ Read-Only HTML Time-Capsules for sharing with editors!
6 Run Your First Scene Turn, Branch "What-Ifs" & Merge Timelines
⏱️ ~2 Minutes

1. Stage Your Cast (Save 60–80% on Tokens!): In the Left Console's Green Room Stage, keep only the characters physically in this scene active (#1, #2) and click off-screen characters to bench them into 💤 Dormant (0 tokens, zero meta-spoilers!).

2. Optional Tension Clock: Add a countdown clock like Border Inspection=1/4(Danger) if the scene has a ticking time-bomb.

3. Write Your Scene Nudge & Run: Write 1–2 sentences describing the physical trigger or event happening right now, then press Ctrl + Enter (or click ▶ Run Turn in Sightline).

4. Branch & Converge: Every turn generates Branch A (Canon) plus dashed Ghost Cards for Branch B (High Chaos) and Branch C (Strategic Twist). Click any Ghost Card with 📌 Branch Selected active to play out an alternate timeline—or click ⑂ (3-Way Branch Merger) on the Left Rail to fuse the best outcomes of two branches into one canonical hub at 0 LLM cost!

📌 Using SIGHTLINE™ Standard? You get full 3-Branch Multiverse turns, Dormant Green Room savings, Tension Clocks, `@world_turn`, and the 3-Way Branch Merger. SIGHTLINE™ PRO supercharges plotting with 🌌 Parallel 2-Branch Runs, 🎴 Salience Storylet Set-Pieces, 📌 Floating Destination Beats + 🎯 Vince Gilligan's Reverse-Causal Bridge Solver, GRRM's Meereenese Knot Arrival Order in the Merger, 🕵️ 1-Click Red-Team This Scene, and 🌊 Downstream Cascade Edits!
