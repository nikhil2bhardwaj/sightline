Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Thank you for choosing SIGHTLINE™ and SIGHTLINE™ PRO. Please review this Refund Policy carefully before purchasing a lifetime license key.

1. Try Before You Buy (7-Day Free Trial)

We provide a full-featured 7-Day Free Trial for both Windows (.exe) and macOS (.dmg) with no credit card required. During your trial, you can test our built-in demos, auto-ingest your own manuscript synopsis, and verify compatibility with your hardware and local AI tools (such as Ollama or LM Studio) prior to purchase.

Because you can evaluate the complete software free of charge for 7 days before buying, we strongly encourage all users to test SIGHTLINE™ on their system before purchasing a lifetime license key.

2. 7-Day Refund Window & Eligibility

All orders for SIGHTLINE™ and SIGHTLINE™ PRO (Standard, PRO, Teams 5-Seat, and Enterprise 20-Seat editions) are processed by our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments, and are subject to the Dodo Payments Buyer Terms.

You may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of your original purchase date under the following conditions:

  • Technical Failure: The application fails to launch or function as advertised on a supported Windows or macOS machine, and our support team cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Accidental Duplicate Purchase: You were unintentionally billed twice for the same license edition.
  • Unactivated License Key: You purchased a license key in error and have not activated it on any computer.

3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will generally not be issued for:

  • Requests submitted more than 7 calendar days after the transaction date.
  • Change of mind after activating and using a lifetime license key.
  • System or local hardware incompatibility that could have been verified during the 7-Day Free Trial.
  • Third-party LLM API charges (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or Groq), which are billed directly by your chosen API provider and are separate from SIGHTLINE™.

4. License Deactivation & Author Data Ownership

Once a refund is approved and processed, all associated lifetime license keys (including all seats in a Teams or Enterprise pack) are permanently revoked. All local manuscripts, Markdown files, .fountain screenplays, World Bibles, and project folders stored on your computer remain 100% your intellectual property to keep.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request within 7 days of purchase, please email mee2me.nb@gmail.com with:

  1. The email address used during checkout.
  2. Your Dodo Payments Order / Transaction ID.
  3. A brief explanation of the issue or reason for your refund request.

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Dodo Payments within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
