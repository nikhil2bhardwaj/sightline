Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SIGHTLINE™ PRO, licensing, security, and offline usage.

1. Is SIGHTLINE™ an AI ghostwriter that writes my prose for me? ▼
No. SIGHTLINE™ is not an AI ghostwriter. It is a standalone native desktop narrative simulation engine, stage-blocking studio, and continuity auditor. Standard AI tools try to write generic, hallucinated prose that flattens your voice. SIGHTLINE™ does the opposite: it models your story world like a physics simulation. It manages room blocking, information asymmetry (what characters physically see and hear), catches plot holes and unfired Chekhov's guns, and compiles state-constrained blueprints that you draft inside the disk-backed Manuscript Studio.
2. Why does SIGHTLINE™ use "Story-in-the-Loop" sensor isolation? ▼
In standard AI chat tools, the entire story is dumped into one shared context window. If Character A hides a weapon in Chapter 2, Character B magically acts like they know about it in Chapter 3 (Omniscient Context Bleed). SIGHTLINE™ was built by a former Mercedes-Benz Hardware-in-the-Loop (HiL) Validation Engineer. Just as automotive HiL testing isolates each ECU and feeds it strictly what its physical sensors can detect, SIGHTLINE™ isolates each character agent and feeds it strictly its own visual and acoustic sightlines across doors, walls, and air vents.
3. How does SIGHTLINE™ slash token costs by 80% to 95%? ▼
Standard AI tools re-send your entire 50-turn chat history on every turn (50,000+ tokens). SIGHTLINE™ is a Markov State Machine: on Turn 100, it sends strictly the single current state snapshot (~700 tokens) plus keyword-triggered lore (≤600 tokens). Turn 100 costs the exact same tiny input tokens as Turn 1! Combined with our 0-token Dormant Green Room (💤) and asymmetric model routing, running a 100,000-word book costs pennies in API fees.
